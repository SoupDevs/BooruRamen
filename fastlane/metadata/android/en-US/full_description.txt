BooruRamen turns image boards into one endless, personalized feed of anime art. Just scroll: every like, skip, favorite and second of watch time quietly teaches the feed what you enjoy, and it reshapes itself around your taste while still showing you something new.

Everything it learns stays on your phone. No account, no sign-up, no ads and no tracking.

<b>A feed that learns as you scroll</b>
• Personalized recommendations computed entirely on your device
• Adapts within the current session and grows smarter over time
• Keeps exploring trending posts you are likely to love, so your feed never goes stale
• "Because you liked…" hints show why a post was picked

<b>Deep Dive</b>
Found something great? Dive into it and the feed fills with closely related posts until you choose to come back up. Your main feed stays balanced.

<b>All your favorite boards in one place</b>
• Mix several image board sites in a single feed
• Add your own compatible sources
• Optional account keys for sites that offer them, stored only on your device

<b>Safe, all-ages content</b>
• The feed shows only general-rated (all-ages) posts
• Block any tag and posts with it are never shown

<b>A proper viewer</b>
• Full-screen images and videos with smooth swiping
• Tags, comments, downloads and sharing
• Optional on-device AI tagging helps the feed understand images beyond their tags. Nothing is uploaded.

<b>Make it yours</b>
• Theme presets or your own colors and fonts
• Separate profiles, each with its own sources, filters and learned taste
• Export a profile to move it to another device
• Analytics of your top tags, favorites and watch time

<b>Open source</b>
BooruRamen is free software under the GPLv3. The source code is on GitHub at github.com/SoupDevs/BooruRamen.

BooruRamen is an independent viewer. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by any of the sites it can browse; all images belong to their respective artists and are loaded directly from the sites that host them.
