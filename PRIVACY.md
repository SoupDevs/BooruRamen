# BooruRamen privacy policy

_Last updated: November 15, 2026_

BooruRamen is a free, open-source app made by SoupDevs. This policy explains
what the app does with your information. In short: **we do not collect,
receive or sell any of your data.** We run no servers, there are no accounts,
no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

## What stays on your device

Everything the app needs to personalize your feed is stored only on your
device, in the app's private storage:

- your likes, skips, favorites, view history and watch time, and the taste
  profile the app learns from them;
- your settings, profiles, sources, blocked tags and themes;
- optional account details (user name and API key) for image board sites
  that offer them;
- images and files you choose to download, saved to your device's Downloads
  folder.

None of this is sent to us. You can export a profile yourself, and you can
delete all of it at any time from the app's settings or by clearing the app's
storage or uninstalling it.

## What the app connects to

To show you posts, the app connects directly from your device to:

- **The image board sites you browse** (the default sources, or ones you
  add). The app sends them the searches it makes for your feed, and your
  account details for that site if you entered any. Like any website, those
  sites see your IP address and the requests made. Their own privacy policies
  apply.
- **Hugging Face** (huggingface.co), only if you turn on on-device AI
  tagging, to download the tagging model once. Images are analysed on your
  device and are never uploaded.
- **GitHub** (github.com), only when you open the plugin browser, to list
  and download what you choose to install.
- **Sharing targets**, only when you tap Share and pick a service; that
  service then receives the link you share.

Some sites protect themselves with a browser check (for example Cloudflare).
When that happens the app opens the site's page so you can pass the check,
and keeps the site's resulting cookie on your device for later requests to
that site.

All connections use encrypted HTTPS where the site supports it.

## Permissions

- **Internet**: to load posts and media.
- **Notifications**: to show download progress.
- **Storage** (Android 10 and older only): to save the files you download.

## Children

BooruRamen is not directed at children under 13. The feed shows only
general-rated posts, but it displays content hosted by third-party sites
that we do not control.
